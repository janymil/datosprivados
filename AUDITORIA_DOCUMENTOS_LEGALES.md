# Audit of the legal documents against Ley N.° 29733 / DS N.° 016-2024-JUS

Date: 31 Oct 2026 · Scope: every document generated by DatosPrivados (`src/lib/documents.ts`, `document-catalog.ts`), the data-bank presets, the sector catalogue and how they connect.

## 1. Short answers

| Your question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Are the documents predefined text? | Yes. They are template functions in `src/lib/documents.ts` that fill in company data (name, RUC, DPO, address) and the declared data banks. |
| Do they adapt to the business sector? | **Not before this audit.** The sector only chose which *data banks* were suggested at onboarding. No document text depended on it, so a clinic, a school and a bakery received the same sensitive-data clause (an empty placeholder), the same retention table and the same Terms. Terms and Cookies were even written for one specific SaaS (they named Kaptia, Clerk, Cloudflare and Google Analytics). **Now fixed**: see §4. |
| 77 business types? | There are **63** (`src/lib/sectors.ts`, 12 groups). All 63 now map to one of 26 legal families (`src/lib/sector-legal.ts`); a script check confirms none is unmapped. |

## 2. Method and sources

- Read the official text of DS N.° 016-2024-JUS (El Peruano, 30 Nov 2024, Congress copy) and checked each legal claim in the templates against its articles.
- Web sources (law-firm summaries, IAPP) were used only to cross-check; where they contradicted the decree text, the decree won. One EY alert on "interés legítimo" turned out to be about **Ecuador**, not Peru, and was discarded.
- Ran all 13 documents for all 63 sectors: no errors, no `undefined`, no leftover Kaptia/Clerk/Neon text, no broken `/reclamaciones/…` link.
- `tsc --noEmit` and `next build` pass.

## 3. Findings (what was wrong) and fixes

### Errors against the law

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | **Legal basis «interés legítimo del responsable»**. Peruvian law has no stand-alone controller legitimate interest like GDPR 6(1)(f); art. 14 of the Law exempts consent for the *data subject's* legitimate interests. Used for CCTV, fraud prevention and logs. | Label rewritten: CCTV rests on prior signage + unequivocal consent by conduct (art. 5.2) and Directiva 01-2020-JUS/DGTAIPD. Security logs now use `obligacion_legal` (art. 46.3 requires them). |
| 2 | High | Sensitive-data clause (art. 8) was a blank placeholder identical for every sector. | Sector-specific purpose/data text; cites art. 8 and 9 (burden of proof is the controller's); signature year fixed (`202___` → `20___`). |
| 3 | High | **Terms of Service** were written for one finance app and generated for every business ("herramienta de software de productividad", plans Standard/Premium, "Kaptia"). | Two variants: platform/SaaS and sale of goods/services (price with IGV, delivery, legal warranty, Libro de Reclamaciones). Financial disclaimer only for finance apps. |
| 4 | High | **Cookie table** listed Clerk, Cloudflare, Google Analytics/Ads for every business. A false cookie table makes the policy untruthful. | Table is blank placeholders + "habitual examples" list; adds that online identifiers are personal data (art. 2.4). |
| 5 | High | **Broken link**: Terms and Libro de Reclamaciones pointed to `/reclamaciones/<slug>`, which does not exist (404). | Placeholder `[enlace de tu Libro de Reclamaciones virtual]` plus a note. Building the hosted Libro is a follow-up (see §6). |
| 6 | Medium | Policy §8 always said "no automated decisions", false for AI/OCR/voice banks. | Conditional text: if the business uses automation it says so (art. 6.1.8), offers human review and the *tratamiento objetivo* right (art. 87). |
| 7 | Medium | Rights list omitted *tratamiento objetivo* (art. 87) and *desindexación* (art. 86.5); portability had no scope. | Added, with art. 76 conditions. |
| 8 | Medium | Transfers: said "verificando niveles adecuados". No assertion that a country is adequate can be made unless the Authority declares it (art. 19); also missed that every transfer must be **communicated to the ANPD** (art. 21.2). | Rewritten per arts. 18–21 (also in the Encargo annex II with the 30-day opinion option and burden of proof, art. 14). |
| 9 | Medium | ARCO text: no mention of 7-day request / 10-day reply / suspension (art. 70), 5+5-day *subsanación* (art. 66.2), gratuity (67.3), art. 73 wording, "Yo cuido mis datos personales". | Added to the policy and to the new ARCO procedure. |
| 10 | Medium | Document de Seguridad cited a "Directiva de Seguridad de la ANPD" tied to the repealed DS 003-2013-JUS and omitted mandatory items: **access logs kept ≥ 2 years (art. 46.3)**, staff training (46.2), approved messaging/e-mail (46.4), secure areas/equipment (48–50), weekly backup with full-restore test (51), how to get *fecha cierta* (47.1, 58), sensitive flag in the inventory (47.3), blocking before deletion. | All added; now cites arts. 46–58. |
| 11 | Medium | `security-checks` cited art. 46.3 for "baja de usuarios" (it is 46.1.a) and had only 4 of the evidence items. | Citation fixed; added checks for access logs, training and backups. |
| 12 | Medium | Encargo clause lacked the **2-year maximum retention** after the last encargo (art. 31.2), subcontracting authorisation (32), subcontractor liability (33), immediate incident notice (36), no ownership of the bank (29.2). The provider annex listed SaaS vendors for every business. | All added; annex now differs for digital vs. ordinary businesses. |
| 13 | Medium | EIPD risk table was SaaS/AI-only; the `ia` regex matched almost any word ("videovigilancia", "historias") so every bank looked high-risk. | Word-boundary regex, sector risk rows, cites art. 40.1–40.2 and **art. 125.4** (an accredited EIPD before a sanction proceeding is a mitigating factor). EIPD correctly stays *facultativa*. |
| 14 | Medium | CCTV: no sign format; no extended notice; schools must keep 30 days max. | Sign: yellow/contrasting, ≥ A4, extended information sheet, protocol, no cameras in toilets/changing rooms, 30 days for schools. |
| 15 | Low | ODP note: ok on dates, missing art. 37.4 (publish contact) and 37.5 (tell ANPD within 15 days). The ODP duty applies by activity: a sector whose main activity is sensitive data is in scope on the revenue calendar. | Acta rewritten. |
| 16 | Low | `MINORS_NOTE` said only parents consent; art. 22.2 lets 14–17-year-olds consent. | Fixed in sectors, consent texts, policy. |
| 17 | Low | `DOCUMENTOS_LEGALES_PERU.md` claimed a "15-day deadline to complain to the ANPD" and 15–30 days CCTV retention. The decree has no such 15-day filing deadline (15 days is the *respondent's* reply time, art. 90). | Corrected. `ai.ts` already told the model not to mention it. |
| 18 | Low | Incident-criteria hint listed the old sensitive-data list. | Updated to art. 2.6 (genetic, biometric, neural, mental/physical health, union membership, family/affective life, intimate habits). |
| 19 | Low | Bank presets cited Clerk/Neon/Stripe/Cloudflare as if every business used them. | Generic wording. |

Checked and **correct as written**: ARCO deadlines 8/10/20 *días hábiles* (arts. 2.8, 69), single extension (71), incident notice in 48 h with the four criteria (34.1), communication to affected people (34.3–34.4), CNSD for digital incidents (34.5), documenting every incident (35), ODP calendar 2025/2026/2027/2028 (First Final Provision), portability in force 6 months after the regulation, written consent for sensitive data (art. 8), 10-day limit for revocation and advertising opt-out (10.2, 26.5), 14-year threshold (22, 25), weekly backups (51.1), 2-year logs (46.3).

## 4. What changed to make documents sector-aware

New module `src/lib/sector-legal.ts`:

- 63 sectors → 26 legal families (health, wellness/gym, pharmacy/optical, schools, adult education, hospitality, passenger transport, courier, food, nightlife, finance, insurance, real estate, vehicles, condominiums, SaaS, fintech app, media, private security, NGO/church, industry/agro, HR agencies, professional services, online retail, general commerce, travel).
- Each family sets: sensitive data, minors, image use, biometrics, SPLAFT exposure, processor role, AI use, sector norms, retention rows, consent clause, security measures, EIPD risk rows, CCTV retention.
- Generators read it: the **Privacy Policy** gets a new §10 "Datos sensibles, menores y normas de nuestra actividad"; **Consent texts** get a sector-specific sensitive clause plus, when relevant, section F (parents / 14–17) and G (image rights, Código Civil art. 15); **Security document** gets sector measures; **Retention** gets a sector table; **EIPD** gets sector risks; **Terms** switch variant; **short notices** switch checkout text.
- The Documents screen now shows, per document, whether it is *esencial / recomendado / opcional for the user's sector* with a one-line reason (`documentFit`).
- The assistant's sector answer ("¿qué necesito?") uses the same profile.
- Data-bank catalogue: two missing presets added (`visitantes`; `creditos` for lenders, exchange houses and other KYC/SPLAFT businesses); Libro de Reclamaciones added to consumer-facing professional sectors (accountants, lawyers, consultants, architects, tradespeople, tailors, builders) that lacked it.

## 5. Documents that were missing and are now written

## 6. Flow analysis (how the documents connect)

```
Registro (sector from landing template) → Onboarding (sector, address, DPO, banks)
   → data_banks (borrador)  → Política + Consentimiento auto-generated
   → /app/documentos  (fit per sector) → generate / publish / sign / store
   → /p/[slug] public policy  ·  /solicitud/[slug] ARCO form → /app/arco (deadlines)
   → /app/incidentes (48 h) · /app/bancos/[id]/ficha (SIPDP) · compliance score
```

What works: sector → banks → policy/consent/security; the policy lists each bank with purpose, basis, retention and recipients; ARCO form and incident module follow the correct deadlines.

Gaps remaining (not changed, need a decision or a feature):

1. **No hosted Libro de Reclamaciones virtual.** Terms and the Libro document now carry a placeholder; a `/reclamaciones/[slug]` form (+ table, + ARCO-like register) is a real feature to build if you want to offer it.
2. **Only the privacy policy is regenerated on bank changes.** Changing the *sector* in `/app/empresa` does not flag any document as outdated; `computeCompliance` only compares bank timestamps to the policy. Consent texts, security, retention and EIPD can drift. Suggest a content hash per document.
3. **The compliance score does not include the new documents** (ARCO procedure, worker notice); I did not re-weight the 100 points. Easy to add if you want them counted.
4. **Onboarding generates only Policy and Consent.** The rest are created on first click, which is fine, but "esencial for your sector" is shown only in the Documents screen, not on the dashboard checklist.
5. The ARCO response templates (`arco-response-form.tsx`) are still the four generic letters; sector exceptions (e.g. clinical records cannot be deleted before the NTS term) could be added.
6. Custom sector text (company.sector not in the catalogue) falls back to the generic "comercio" profile.
7. No DPA/ROPA export document and no privacy-training log beyond the form in the worker notice.

## 7. What I could not fully verify (have a lawyer confirm)

Verified directly against the decree: everything cited as "art. N del DS 016-2024-JUS". **Cited from memory or secondary sources, not read in the primary text during this audit**:

- Ley 29733 art. 14 numerals (I avoided numbers other than 14.1 and 14.5, already in the code) and the wording that "ingresos económicos" is sensitive in the Law (art. 2.5), whereas the new Regulation's art. 2.6 list omits it. I treated income data conservatively, with a note.
- Directiva 01-2020-JUS/DGTAIPD details (30–60 days, 30 for schools, yellow sign ≥ 297×210 mm, extended notice) come from law-firm summaries; the 2-business-day deletion rule was left out.
- Sector norms: Ley 26842, Ley 29414, Ley 30024, NTS 139-MINSA/2018 (RM 214-2018/MINSA; retention years deliberately *not* stated), D. Leg. 1490, Ley 30947, Ley 29459 / DS 014-2011-SA, Ley 28044, Ley 29719, DS 001-2015-MINCETUR, DS 017-2009-MTC, Ley 28681, Ley 27157 / DS 008-2000-MTC, D. Leg. 1213, Ley 29946, Ley 26702, Ley 30822, Ley 27489, Ley 29783, Ley 31814, Ley 27693 / Ley 29038 / DS 020-2017-JUS (10-year record keeping confirmed by an SBS source), DS 011-2011-PCM, Opinión Consultiva 045-2021-JUS/DGTAIPD.
- Which businesses are *sujetos obligados* to the UIF is phrased as "verify against the SBS list".
- Tax retention of 5 years (4-year prescription + margin) is kept from the original presets.

These documents remain drafts; an advisor should review the sensitive-data and minors clauses before publication, especially for health and education.

## 10. Update 2026-10-31: tools, data location, evidence and sector protocol

Added after the first audit (all of it driven by the same sector profiles):

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Template versions + outdated detection | `document-freshness.ts` | Each saved document stores the template version and a fingerprint of company, sector, banks and tools. Changes flag it "Desactualizado"; one click regenerates all. |
| Tool catalogue | `provider-catalog.ts` | 24 categories, ~170 common tools with a suggested country; every category accepts a typed tool. Categories specific to a rubro (clinic software, aula virtual, OTA/booking, POS, gym access, property portals, cameras, job boards, credit bureaus). |
| Onboarding step "Herramientas" + page `/app/herramientas` | `tools-picker.tsx`, `actions/tools.ts` | Tick tools, set the country where each stores data, add your own. Per tool: contract (DPA) status and "comunicado a la ANPD" status. |
| Documents use the tools | `documents.ts` | Policy §6 lists providers and destination countries and the transfer text follows them; cookie table is built from the declared tools; encargo annex, security inventory and EIPD list them. |
| Sales band, website, Libro link | onboarding and `/app/empresa` | Sales band drives the exact ODP date; website enables web categories; the Libro link (e.g. librodigital.pe) replaces the placeholder in Terms and the Libro document. |
| Evidence register | `/app/evidencias` | Consent, training, access review, backup test and purge entries with date and proof reference. |
| Protocol of your rubro | document `sectorial` | Daily procedures, norms, retention, typical risks and tools for the 26 legal families. |
| Compliance | `compliance.ts` | New scored item "Declara las herramientas" (weights still sum to 100). Warning alerts, not penalised: foreign tools without contract/ANPD communication, banks saying "no transfer" while foreign tools exist, SIPDP sheet outdated after changes (art. 44), ODP date approaching, access review older than six months. |

Caveats: the suggested countries are defaults to confirm in each provider account (where unsure the catalogue says "No sé", treated as a transfer to verify); the list of countries the ANPD considers adequate is not hardcoded.
